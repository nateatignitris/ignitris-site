RD Silk Gold Re-Set is an advanced leave-in hair treatment formulated with silk protein to instantly soften and revitalize damaged, dry, and chemically treated hair. Its lightweight, non-greasy formula absorbs quickly without weighing hair down.

  • Silk protein penetrates deep to replenish nutrients in damaged hair
  • Forms a moisture barrier on the hair surface for lasting hydration
  • Adds elasticity and natural shine to thin, fragile hair
  • Non-greasy formula absorbs quickly — no residue or buildup
  • Apply before heat styling for added protection and hydration
  • Suitable for all hair types, especially damaged and color-treated

Daily Leave-In Care

After shampooing, towel-dry hair leaving slight moisture. Dispense a small amount into your palm, spread evenly through mid-lengths and ends. Do not rinse. Style as desired.

Deep Treatment

Moisten hair and scalp thoroughly. Apply product evenly from roots to tips. Leave on for 2–3 minutes, then rinse with lukewarm water for a deeper conditioning treatment.

Heat Protection

Apply a small amount to damp or dry hair before blow-drying, flat ironing, or curling for added heat protection and hydration.
