As a leave-in: after shampooing, towel-dry hair so it is still slightly damp. Smooth a small amount through mid-lengths and ends, then dry and style as usual. No need to rinse.

As a treatment: apply evenly to damp hair, leave on for 2–3 minutes, then rinse with lukewarm water.

Fine hair? Start with a smaller amount.

Do I need to rinse it out?
No. It's a no-rinse leave-in, so you can apply it and style as usual. For a deeper rinse-off treatment, leave it on for 2–3 minutes and rinse.
Will it make my hair greasy?
The formula is non-greasy and absorbs quickly. If your hair is fine, use a small amount.
Can I use it on color-treated or permed hair?
Yes. It's made for damaged and chemically treated hair.
How much is in the tube?
180 ml (6.08 fl oz).
