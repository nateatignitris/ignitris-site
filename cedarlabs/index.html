The Science of Skin After 40

These fine lines? This loss of firmness? These dark spots?

They look like three different problems. But there's something happening under your skin that most women never hear about.

Women come into my office all the time and vent about the creams, the serums, the ice rollers they bought on Amazon that didn't change a single thing. Everything social media told them to buy, they bought. And when nothing changed, they felt lost.

And the part that makes me the most mad? They're getting this advice from women on social media who are clearly in their twenties.

"Honey, our bodies aren't even the same anymore."

Look, there's nothing wrong with natural aging. But as our bodies change, how we take care of them needs to change, too.

And that's when I introduce them to something called NAD+.

It's something our bodies naturally make that helps our cells function and repair themselves — which includes the skin cells in your face.

The issue is that NAD+ levels take a dramatic hit when we turn 40. So we're spending all this money on the outside without thinking about what's changing underneath.

And here's the hard part: after 40, we have no natural way of increasing our NAD+ levels. We can't get it from food, and there's no NAD+ supplement that delivers it either.
